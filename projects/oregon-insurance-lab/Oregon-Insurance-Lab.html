Keep these straight

Five terms that do the work

Primary
Payer billed first for this service. The order can change with employment, injury, or the type of coverage.
Secondary
Considers a claim after the primary payer. It does not promise to pay the entire remainder.
Eligibility
Whether coverage is active on the date of service. A physical card alone does not prove this.
CCO / Open Card
Two ways OHP coverage is administered. MMIS shows the current enrollment and benefit package.
MSPQ
Medicare Secondary Payer questions identify situations where another payer may come before Original Medicare.
